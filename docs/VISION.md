# LabourLink — Vision

## What It Is

LabourLink is a labour management system built for greenhouse operations.
It runs entirely on local hardware inside the greenhouse network.

Its purpose is to give greenhouse managers a single, reliable place to:

- Record who works, when, and on what
- Track where in the greenhouse labour is performed (row and location level)
- Register yield and carrier data at the point of work
- Generate payroll-ready output from recorded data
- Understand productivity across employees, activities, and locations

## Who It Is For

- **Greenhouse managers** who need to register and review daily labour
- **Payroll administrators** who need accurate, auditable output
- **Team leads** who allocate employees to tasks and locations
- **Operations staff** who register labour and yield from a mobile device

## What Problem It Solves

Greenhouses often manage labour through paper, spreadsheets, or generic HR tools
that were not designed for row-level agricultural work. This creates:

- Manual payroll calculation prone to error
- No connection between location, activity, and productivity
- No audit trail when disputes arise
- Lost data when spreadsheets are overwritten

LabourLink replaces this with a structured, event-based system where everything
is recorded once and never lost.

## What It Is Not

- Not a cloud SaaS product
- Not a general HR system
- Not dependent on internet connectivity
- Not a financial accounting system (it produces payroll input, not payroll itself)

## Long-Term Direction

The system is designed in layers:

1. **Foundation** — master data, migration framework, layout
2. **Employee module** — employees, contracts, employment history
3. **Input module** — daily labour registration, breaks, yield
4. **Reporting module** — payroll output, productivity summaries
5. **Android app** — mobile registration at the work location

Each layer builds on a stable foundation. Features are never rushed.
