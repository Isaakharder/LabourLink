LabourLink Support

Help for employees and administrators using the LabourLink app on iPhone, Android and the web.

Contact support

Email support@lltech.io. Include your workplace name, the phone model, and what you were doing when the problem happened. If your question is about your hours, jobs or employee details, your employer's LabourLink administrator can usually help fastest.

Pairing a phone

Each phone that uses the LabourLink app is paired to one employee by an administrator.

  1. Install LabourLink and open it. The app shows a 6-digit pairing code.
  2. Give the code to your administrator. They approve it on the Setup page of the LabourLink desktop site and choose which employee the phone belongs to.
  3. The phone switches to the home screen by itself within a few seconds of approval.
  • The code expired. Codes last 10 minutes. Close and reopen the app to get a new one.
  • New or replacement phone. Pair the new phone the same way. Your administrator can deactivate the old phone from the desktop site.
  • "Have an access code?" Access codes are only for app reviewers and demonstrations. They connect the app to a separate demo with fictional data, not to a real workplace. Employees should always pair with the 6-digit code instead.

Working offline and syncing

  • LabourLink keeps recording work start, breaks, job changes and end of day without a connection. These show as "pending sync" until the phone is back online.
  • Pending records are sent automatically when the connection returns or the app is opened again. Settings shows the sync status and has a Sync now button.
  • Don't uninstall the app while records are pending: records that haven't been sent yet are only on the phone.

Notifications

  • Open Settings in the app and tap Enable notifications, then allow notifications when your phone asks.
  • Notifications only tell you a new message is waiting; open LabourLink to read it.
  • If nothing arrives, check that notifications for LabourLink are allowed in your phone's own settings.

NFC row and bin tags

  • iPhone: tap Scan, then hold the top of the iPhone near the tag until it confirms.
  • Android: hold the tag near the back of the phone while the job screen is open.
  • NFC is optional. You can always choose the row or bin from the list instead.

Signing in on the web

  • Administrators and managers sign in to the LabourLink website with their email address and PIN.
  • "Forgot PIN?" emails a reset link to the address on your employee profile. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes.

Privacy and your data

Read the LabourLink Privacy Policy for what information LabourLink collects, how it is used, and how to ask for access, correction or deletion.
