LabourLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 7, 2026

LabourLink is a workforce time-tracking app for greenhouse and agricultural operations. It is developed and operated by Isaak Harder Hiebert, operating as LinkLogic Technologies, based in Ontario, Canada ("LinkLogic Technologies", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the LabourLink mobile app, web app and related services collect, how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and the choices available to you.

Summary

  • LabourLink is used by employers to record their employees' working time. Employee accounts are created by the employer, not by the public.
  • We collect employee profile details, work and time records, and limited device information needed to pair a phone and send notifications.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, we do not track your location, and we do not sell personal information.
  • Information is shared with the employer that uses LabourLink, on workplace displays and integrations that employer sets up, and with the service providers that run LabourLink.

1. Who this policy covers

LabourLink is published by LinkLogic Technologies and is used by employers (for example, greenhouse operators) to record their own staff's working time. Your employer is a separate organization from LinkLogic Technologies.

If you use LabourLink because your employer has set you up in it, your employer decides which information about you is entered into LabourLink and how it is used for employment purposes. LinkLogic Technologies stores and processes that information to provide the service to your employer. Your employer may have its own privacy policy that also applies to you.

2. Information we collect

Employee and account information

An authorized administrator at your employer enters this information. Depending on what your employer chooses to record, it may include:

  • First and last name, employee number, email address and phone number.
  • Gender, date of birth, nationality and preferred language.
  • Profile photo.
  • Role and permission level, team role, job group, employee group and activity groups.
  • Employment details such as start date, employment periods, expected and actual finish dates, employment type, work group and administrator notes.
  • Work permit expiry date, a history of changes to it, and reminder settings for upcoming expiries.
  • A sign-in PIN. The PIN is stored only in hashed (one-way encrypted) form and is never stored in readable form.

Work and time records

  • Work start, break start, break end, activity changes and end-of-day events, each with a date and time and the time-zone offset of the device.
  • The activity being performed and, where applicable, the greenhouse row, carrier (cart or bin) and plant-density information used to calculate work speed.
  • Row and carrier completions, work speeds and totals calculated from the records above.
  • Manual entries, corrections and deletions made by administrators, including who made the change, when, and the reason given.
  • Information about how each record was synchronized from a phone, including sync status and any conflicts reviewed by an administrator.

Messages

  • Messages sent by administrators to employees, and when each message was delivered and acknowledged.

Device pairing information

Each phone that uses the LabourLink mobile app is paired to an employee by an administrator. For this we collect:

  • A random device identifier that the app creates on the phone the first time it runs. It is not your phone's hardware serial number, IMEI or advertising ID.
  • A short pairing code shown on the phone, and the time and administrator who approved the pairing.
  • A device name entered by the administrator, the app version, and when the device last connected and last synchronized.

Notification information

  • If notifications are enabled, a push notification token (Android) or web push subscription (web browsers). This lets us tell the device that a new message is waiting.

Sign-in and password reset

  • When you sign in to the web app, a session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours.
  • If you request a PIN reset, we create a single-use reset link that expires after 30 minutes and email it to the address on your employee profile.

3. Device permissions and features

  • NFC. The app uses NFC only while a work or administration screen that needs it is open. It reads or writes tags attached to greenhouse rows and carriers so the right row or carrier can be selected. Only the tag's identifier is used. NFC is not used to collect information about you, and the app works without NFC through manual selection.
  • Notifications. On Android 13 and later, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Notifications only say that a new message is waiting; the message itself is shown inside the app. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Vibration. Used for short confirmation feedback, for example after a tag is scanned.
  • Internet and network status. Used to send records to the LabourLink server and to know when the device is offline.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Information stored on your device

So that time can be recorded without a network connection, LabourLink stores the following on the device:

  • Time events (work start, breaks, activity changes, end of day) waiting to be sent to the server, along with their sync status.
  • A copy of reference data such as activities, greenhouse rows, carriers and NFC tag mappings.
  • The device identifier, whether the device is paired, and a short summary of the paired employee (name, profile photo link and preferred language).

This information is sent only to the LabourLink server. Where it is kept depends on how you use LabourLink:

  • Android app. The information is kept in the app's private storage on the phone. The app is set so that this storage is excluded from Android device backups and device-to-device transfers. Uninstalling the app removes it from the phone.
  • Web browser (including a LabourLink shortcut or icon added to a home screen). The information is kept in that browser's storage for the LabourLink website. Uninstalling the Android app does not affect it, and removing a home-screen shortcut or icon may not remove it either. You can remove it by clearing the browser's site data for LabourLink. Signing in to the web app also stores a session cookie in the browser.

Records that have already been sent to the server are not affected by uninstalling the app or clearing browser data.

5. How we use information

  • To record and calculate working time, breaks, activities and work speed for your employer, including for payroll, scheduling and reporting.
  • To let employees and supervisors see current work status, for example on greenhouse displays and dashboards set up by the employer.
  • To pair phones with employees and keep records accurate when a phone works offline.
  • To deliver messages and notifications from your employer.
  • To remind authorized administrators about upcoming work permit expiry dates.
  • To sign you in, reset your PIN and protect the service against unauthorized access.
  • To diagnose and fix problems with the service.

6. How information is shared

  • Your employer. Authorized administrators and supervisors at your employer can view and manage the information described above, according to their role in LabourLink.
  • Workplace displays. If your employer sets up greenhouse TV displays, they may show employees' names, profile photos, current activity, row and carrier at the workplace.
  • Employer-authorized integrations. If your employer enables it, a connected workplace display system can receive employee names and work-speed figures through a secure access token. No pay-related information is included.
  • Service providers that host and operate LabourLink for us, described below.
  • Legal reasons. Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of users, our customers or the public.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software in the app.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

9. Data retention

LabourLink does not automatically delete records. Information is kept on our systems until it is deleted manually, and LabourLink does not currently set a fixed retention period.

  • Deactivation is not deletion. When an employee leaves, the employer deactivates their account. This stops the account from being used, but the employee's profile, work and time records, messages and related history are kept and remain visible to authorized administrators.
  • Deleted time entries are kept as an audit trail. When an administrator deletes or corrects a time entry, the original is kept and marked as deleted or corrected, along with who made the change, when, and the reason given. Changes to employment details and work permit dates are also kept as history.
  • Phones. Unpairing or deactivating a phone does not delete the phone's record or the time events it already sent.
  • Profile photos. An administrator can remove a profile photo, which deletes the photo file from storage. Uploading a new photo deletes the previous one.
  • Short-lived codes and links. Pairing codes stop working after 10 minutes, PIN-reset links after 30 minutes, and web sign-in sessions after 12 hours.
  • Information on your device is removed from an Android phone when the LabourLink app is uninstalled. Information stored by a web browser stays until that browser's site data is cleared (see section 4). Records already sent to the server are not affected either way.
  • Service providers may keep backup copies and system logs for periods set by their own practices.

10. Access, correction and deletion requests

To ask for access to the personal information LabourLink holds about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, email support@lltech.io. You can also ask your employer's LabourLink administrator.

Because your employer decides what is recorded about you and may have its own legal obligations, LinkLogic Technologies reviews each request in coordination with the relevant employer. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

  • Access and correction. Your employer's administrators can view and correct your profile and time records in LabourLink.
  • Deletion. LabourLink does not currently have a self-service or automated way to delete an employee and their records. Approved deletion requests are handled manually by LinkLogic Technologies.

Information about an employee is not only in their profile. Handling an approved deletion request means reviewing the employee's profile and profile photos, work and time records, corrections and change history, employment and work permit history, messages, saved reports, device records, and other related records, including records where the employee appears as the person who made or approved a change.

  • A profile photo can be fully deleted.
  • Other information may be deleted where that is possible and permitted. Some records may need to be kept, for example where your employer is required to keep records of hours worked, or where records are linked to other people's records.
  • Removing your name and contact details from your profile does not by itself make the remaining records anonymous. We will describe information that is kept as de-identified only after checking that the remaining records can no longer identify you.

We will tell you the outcome of your request, including any information that was kept and why.

LabourLink accounts are created by employers, and the app has no public sign-up.

11. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and the server.
  • PINs stored only as one-way hashes, and single-use, time-limited reset links.
  • Role-based access, so that people see and change only what their role allows.
  • Phones must be approved by an administrator before they can record time, and can be deactivated at any time.
  • Profile photos stored privately and shown only through short-lived links.

No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children

LabourLink is a workplace tool for employers and their staff. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children outside of an employment relationship set up by an employer.
